## 3) Style Guide (Calm Design, 2026)

### Color Palette
- **Trust Blue**: #1E5AA8 (primary buttons, headings)
- **Growth Green**: #2E9D63 (success states, highlights)
- **Neutral Grey**: #6C7078 (text, body)
- **White Space**: large padding, minimal borders

### Typography
- **Primary Font (Heading):** “Poppins” (modern, clean)
- **Secondary Font (Body):** “Noto Sans Devanagari” (Hindi readability)

### UI/UX Guidelines
- Full-width edges with white space
- Rounded corners (12px)
- Soft drop shadows
- Microcopy in Hinglish (“SIP se wealth build karo”) to sound natural
- CTA buttons: Bold, high contrast

## 4) Interactive Tools Logic (Hindi-first)

### A. SIP Calculator
- Inputs: Monthly Investment, Years, Expected Return
- Output: Total Value, Invested Amount, Returns
- Microcopy: “Aapka SIP future me itna ban sakta hai”

### B. Home Loan EMI Calculator
- Inputs: Loan Amount, Tenure, Interest Rate
- Output: EMI, Total Interest, Total Payable
- Microcopy: “Apka monthly EMI itna hoga”

### C. Tax Bachao Wizard
- Step 1: Income range
- Step 2: 80C / 80D spending
- Step 3: Family details
- Output: Best tax-saving option (ELSS, PPF, Insurance)
- Microcopy: “Aap itna tax bacha sakte hain”
